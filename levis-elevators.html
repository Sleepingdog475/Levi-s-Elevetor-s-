HOW A PROJECT RUNS

From site visit to first ride

Five stages, the same project manager throughout. You'll know the schedule and the cost before any equipment is ordered.

01

Site survey

An engineer measures the shaft or building plans, checks load requirements, and confirms what's actually possible on site.

1–2 days
02

Design & quote

We spec the car size, drive system, and finishes, then send a fixed quote with a delivery and install timeline.

3–5 days
03

Manufacturing & delivery

Your unit is built to spec and delivered to site, coordinated with your builder or facilities team to avoid downtime.

6–10 weeks
04

Installation

Our licensed technicians install and wire the system, then run full load and safety testing before handover.

1–3 weeks
05

Certification & handover

Independent inspection, certification paperwork, and a walkthrough with your building manager or homeowner.

2–3 days
